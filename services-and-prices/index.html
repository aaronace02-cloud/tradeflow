breathwork coaching

Walk and Talk

Recognising that some individuals find it easier to express their thoughts and emotions while in motion, Walk and Talk sessions are offered as a holistic approach. These sessions are perfect for those who enjoy being outdoors or want to incorporate exercise and healthy habits into their therapy - a dual-purpose experience. Walk and Talk sessions are available starting from the second session.


This approach is ideal for individuals who prefer brief, movement-oriented sessions and seek support to boost or sustain their motivation for positive change.

Price:

$45 per 30 mins
