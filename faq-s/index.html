FAQ's

  • How to book a session

    Booking is one easy step. Send a message using the 'contact us' feature stating the counselling type and preferred time. 

  • How to cancel a session booking

    Please email any cancellation to: sarah@talk2heal.com.au

  • How is my privacy protected

    Emails and files are all protected and continually monitored.

  • How long is a session

    Usually 60 or 90 minutes sessions. Alternate times can be discussed.

  • How do I contact the counsellor / coach

    Using the contact page.

  • Will my counsellor / coach tell other people what I share with them

    Not at all, unless I am legislated to share your details.

  • Can I receive free counselling sessions with a Mental Health Care Plan

    No. Our counselling sessions are not covered by Medicare.

  • CAN I CHOOSE EITHER COUNSELLING OR BREATHWORK SESSIONS

    Absolutely. You choose the service right for you. Counselling, Breathwork or a bit of both.
